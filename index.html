Rules of the Kingdom

Ride safe, ride happy

A few simple rules keep everyone smiling and our animals in tip-top shape.

  1. Adults superviseAll children must have adult supervision while riding.
  2. No bumpingNo bumping into people or objects.
  3. Stay in the riding areaStay within the designated riding areas.
  4. No standing or jumpingNo standing, jumping or horseplay on the ride-on animal.
  5. No food or drinksNo food or drinks while using Animal Rides Kingdom scooters.
  6. No stairs, stores or upstairsNo riding outside the designated route, including second floors, in stores, on stairs or outside the venue.
  7. Return it with careReturn the ride-on in the same condition you received it.
Big Animal Scooters
125 kg / 275 lbs max

No more than two persons per ride.

Small Animal Scooters
25 kg / 55 lbs max

Limited to one child only.

Important: Riding Surface

For a smooth ride, scooters must be used on flat, hard surfaces only, like pavement, gym floors or concrete.

Not suitable for grass, gravel or uneven terrain.
